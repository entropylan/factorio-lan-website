## Is there Internet?

We have a combination of VDSL, Starlink and 5G to provide around 450mbps of bandwidth. We also use [LanCache](https://lancache.net) to speed up game downloads.

## Is alcohol allowed?

Yes. The venue is not licensed, so you can bring your own alcohol. Please drink
responsibly.

## Who is running this event?

We're [Jessica Smith](https://mintopia.net/about-me/) and
[Chris Rimmer](https://x.com/Cwis_smileyface).

Jessica has been LANning for decades and provides technical infrastructure,
networking gear, and software for LAN parties. She co-founded [Epic.LAN](https://www.epiclan.co.uk), who
currently run the largest LAN party in the country, and is a co-owner of UK
LANS, the current operators of [StratLAN](https://stratlan.net). She is also one of the core developers
of the world famous [LANCache project](https://lancache.net), used by LAN party organisers the world
over to cache game downloads and save precious bandwidth at LANs. Jessica
doesn't just provide infrastructure, she _is_ infrastructure.

Chris, has been playing Factorio a long time, has been to many LAN parties and
even went to the Space Age LAN Party! In his spare time he likes to [repair and build cars](https://pedalbox.show). It turns out that building a car requires more than just 8 engine units, 20 iron plate and 5 steel plate.
