## Is there Internet?

We have a combination of VDSL, Starlink and 5G to provide around 450mbps of bandwidth. We also use [LanCache](https://lancache.net) to speed up game downloads.

## Is alcohol allowed?

Yes. The venue is not licensed, so you can bring your own alcohol. Please drink
responsibly.

## Who is running this event?

We're [Jess Smith](https://mintopia.net/about-me/) and
[Chris Rimmer](https://x.com/Cwis_smileyface).

Jess has been LANning for decades and provides technical infrastructure,
networking gear, software (such as seat pickers and more) for LAN parties. She
co-founded Epic.LAN, who currently run the largest LAN party in the country, and
is a co-owner of UK LANS, the current operators of StratLAN. She is also one of
the core developers of the world famous LANCache project, used by LAN party
organisers the world over to cache game downloads and save precious bandwidth at
LANs. Jess doesn't just provide infrastructure, she _is_ infrastructure.

Chris, meanwhile, is just a guy who goes to a lot of LAN parties.
